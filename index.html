Projects

Translating individual stories into tangible forms of craft.

Baby Moriyama

An algorithmic adaptation of decentralized housing for the steep, high-density topography of Seoul. By internalizing movement through a minimal-surface floating alley, it secures spatial freedom and privacy within a singular urban organism.

Bézier Yoshimoto

A parametric reinterpretation of the Yoshimoto Cube, utilizing Bézier surfaces and waffle structures to rationalize complex kinematics. This computational experiment transforms a 1971 invention into a seamless topography for human comfort.

ANDJ's Nametag

A kinetic object born from the geometry of a name, balancing Pythagorean rigor with interpretive utility. This birch-crafted vessel evolves through time, inviting users to discover the concealed narrative within its arcs and lines.

Food Innovation Hub

Proposal for a hub where various chefs and ingredient suppliers can collaborate to develop unique street foods, embodying the distinctive characteristics in Seoul Central Market.
