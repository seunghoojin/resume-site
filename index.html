01 — About

Hi. My name is Andrew Jin. Call me AndJ for short.

Who I am

I’m an architecture and artificial intelligence student interested in the principles shared by buildings, systems, objects, and stories.

Stories

I believe this world consists of stories—in a very strict sense. My work translates individual narratives into tangible forms of craft.

Building

To confront, innovate, and create—these are words I live by. Inspired by the lyrics of a song. Buildings can be all three at once.

Things I love

Books, fashion, music, coffee, and humans—not all of them. Some things simply deserve attention.
